Terms of Use

This guest network is provided for lawful, considerate use. By connecting, you agree to:

  • Respect other guests and avoid excessive use that affects the network.

YOUR CONNECTION

Privacy Notice

The network operator processes the device and authentication information needed to provide guest access. Depending on the configured sign-in method, this may include your device address, account details, phone number, and connection records.

This page does not store your password in your browser. The network operator manages logging, retention, and access policies. Ask your host for the operator's full privacy policy and contact details.

Guest Wi-Fi does not by itself encrypt your browsing. Use HTTPS websites and avoid sharing sensitive information on untrusted connections.
